## Our own frame device: the window envelope

German business mail comes in a window envelope (*Fensterumschlag*). That's where every negotiation starts: a letter arrives. The presenter appears **inside the address window** of a cream envelope on paper, like the recipient's address. Opening shot: the envelope slides in. Closing shot: the envelope turns over and shows our name. It belongs to our story and to no one else's brand.

## Recording checklist (about 20 minutes)

- Phone in **landscape**, 1080p or 4K. On iPhone, turn on Settings → Camera → Record Video → "Show PAL Formats" and record at **25 fps**.
- Plain **light wall** behind you (white or cream), so the window cut-out works without masking.
- Window light from the front, camera at eye level, framed head to mid-chest, a little space above the head.
- A plain dark top. No logos.
- Clip-on mic if anyone has one; otherwise phone close, quiet room, no echo.
- Read each block P1–P7 two or three times; we pick the best take. Leave a second of silence before and after each take.
- Put the files in `video/demo-60s/source/`, which is git-ignored because of the size.
