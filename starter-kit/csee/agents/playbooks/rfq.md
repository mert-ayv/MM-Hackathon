# RFQ: competition and complete packages

Compare bidders using complete packages, standard terms and must-haves. A lower unit price can lose
on freight, delivery, quality, tooling or coverage. Use current rival evidence, never invent a rival.
Revealing a rival's offer is a strategic decision: check that it actually improves on the recipient's.

Discover what each bidder values and test conditional trades. Cash, lead time, contract length and
volume have different values for different suppliers; avoid category stereotypes without evidence.

Keeping another valid offer can make further bargaining less risky, but an early agreement is not
proof that more concessions are available. Choose when to award based on all live offers, supplier
responses and time, rather than a fixed anchor percentage or number of rounds.

Only the first standing sheet can be accepted. Propose an attractive alternative sheet back unchanged
and obtain confirmation before accepting it. Never let an attractive price conceal a must-have failure.
