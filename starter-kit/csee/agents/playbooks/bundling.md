# Playbook: bundling (several parts as one kit, one supplier)

**What we sell the supplier:** the whole kit's volume in one multi-year contract, instead of today's
split across several suppliers, and simpler logistics (one kit, scheduled call-offs). A distributor with
idle capacity values that most: in P07 Leitner's second shift had been idle since spring and the kit was
about 4.7x its business with us. Say it - our template e-mails never did.

**Our fallback is today's terms.** Walking away keeps the current contract (its C is "our alternative"),
so any deal must beat it; the supplier knows we can stay where we are.

**Offer length, keep the volume.** The longest contract (36 months) and the full volume cost us nothing
and are worth a lot to the supplier; trade them for price. Faster payment is a second trade if the
profile shows cash pressure.

**Expect small steps.** Family distributors often need a committee for moves above about 2 %, so they
concede around 1 % a round (P07: 5.74 -> 5.61 -> 5.55 -> 5.49 -> final 5.45). Keep our own steps small
and steady; a jump on our side only gives money away.

**Avoid:** threats to drop a supplier that is already the cheapest and wants the work (win-win framing
works), and rising-cost arguments - a rising index "confirms their case".

**Evidence:** P07 house agent 0.78; our rules 0.72 (deal EUR 5.45, best reachable about EUR 5.02).
